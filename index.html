Swim

Swim workouts, a poolside rep counter and a swim log for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Know what to swim today, count every rep at the wall, and keep a log that stays yours. The log, the workout builder, poolside mode and the Apple Watch app are free.

What it does

  • Gives you a workout of the day and 16 starter workouts, sized to your pool
  • Counts reps and shows the send-off clock and rest timer, on your phone at the wall or on your Watch
  • Keeps a log of every swim, with your totals and personal bests
  • Lets you build and save your own workouts
  • Exports your whole log as a spreadsheet file
  • Offers the full workout library and multi-week plans as an optional subscription
