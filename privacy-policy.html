Privacy Policy

Swim, the swim workout and swim log app.

Last updated 8 October 2026

Your swims stay on your devices. Swim has no account and no server. Your workouts and your log are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your swim log, the workouts you build, your pool size, your pace and your settings. These are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad, and in your device's own backups if you have those on.
  • A swim recorded on Apple Watch is kept on the Watch until your iPhone has received it. The two talk to each other directly, through Apple's own link between a Watch and its iPhone.
  • The reminders you choose are scheduled on your device. They are local notifications; no push server is involved.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Swim reads your heart rate and the lengths and distance your Apple Watch counts while a swim is being recorded, to show them to you during and after the swim. It saves each recorded swim to Health as a pool swimming workout. You can change or withdraw this permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Health information is used only to show you your own swims. It is not sent to us, is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to us. If you export your log or share a workout, the file or text goes only where you choose to send it. Subscriptions are bought through Apple; we never see your payment details, and Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

Usage information, advertising and tracking

There is none. The app has no analytics, no advertising identifier, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites. We have no way to know who uses the app.

Deleting your data

Delete a swim or a workout in the app and it is gone from the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device, so export your log first if you want a copy. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date above.
