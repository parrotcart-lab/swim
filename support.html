Swim, the swim workout and swim log app.

Email parrotcart@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened, and which iPhone or Apple Watch you use.

Changing your pool

Tap the pool size at the top of Today, or open Settings, and choose 25 yards, 25 metres or 50 metres. Workouts are resized to the pool you pick.

Swimming with Apple Watch

Open a workout on your iPhone and send it to your Watch, then start it on the Watch at the pool. The Watch counts lengths and taps your wrist when it is time to go. Each swim is saved on the Watch first and arrives in your log when the iPhone is next in reach.

A swim from the Watch has not arrived

Open Swim on your iPhone with the Watch nearby and Bluetooth on. A swim that was saved on the Watch is kept there until the iPhone has it.

The distance looks wrong

On the Watch, the saved screen has Fix distance right after a swim. In your Log you can delete a swim and add it again by hand with the right distance and time.

Apple Health

Swims recorded on the Watch are saved to Health if you allow it. To change that, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Swim.

Reminders

Choose your swim days in Settings. If no reminder arrives, check that notifications are allowed for Swim in your iPhone's Settings.

Subscription

The log, the workout builder, poolside mode and the Watch app are free. The full library, plans, filters and send-offs at your own pace are a subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through Apple. Cancel or change it in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. On a new device, tap Restore purchases on the plans screen.

Keeping a copy of your log

In Settings, choose Export your log. Your log lives only on your device, so export it before deleting the app.
